Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 7, 2026

merlion transportation ltd ("we", "our", or "us") publishes games and applications, including the game Tap Game, under the Google Play developer account farm_Dev. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use our games, how it is used, and which third-party services we integrate.

1. Information Collected Through Third-Party SDKs

We do not directly ask users to provide personal information such as name, email, phone number, address, contacts, photos, or payment data. However, our games integrate third-party software development kits (SDKs) that may automatically collect certain technical and usage information for the purposes of advertising, analytics, and offerwall functionality. These SDKs are:

  • Google AdMob – serves in-app advertisements.
  • Google Firebase Analytics – measures app usage and performance.
  • Torox Offerwall – provides an in-app offerwall where users can complete offers and tasks in exchange for in-game rewards.

2. Categories of Data Collected by SDKs

The above SDKs may automatically collect, process, or share the following categories of information:

  • Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / GAID, App Set ID, or similar identifiers)
  • Device information (model, manufacturer, operating system version, language, time zone)
  • Approximate (non-precise) location derived from IP address
  • IP address
  • App usage data (sessions, screens, events, in-app interactions, crashes)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, ad performance)
  • Offerwall data (offers viewed, started, and completed; reward events; anonymous user ID assigned by the offerwall provider)

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise GPS location, contacts, photos, files, or payment information.

3. How the Data Is Used

  • To display advertisements within the game (including personalized or non-personalized ads, depending on user consent and region).
  • To operate the offerwall, validate offer completions, prevent fraud, and grant in-game rewards.
  • To analyze app usage, fix bugs, improve performance, and develop new features.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and invalid traffic.

4. Advertising and Personalized Ads

Google AdMob may use device identifiers and ad-interaction data to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements. Where required by applicable law (such as the GDPR in the EEA/UK), the user will be presented with a consent prompt (Google's User Messaging Platform / IAB TCF) and may choose whether to allow personalized advertising. On Android, users can also reset or limit their Advertising ID through their device settings.

5. Offerwall

The Torox Offerwall is an optional feature. When a user opens the offerwall, the SDK and the third-party offers presented within it may collect device and usage information in order to deliver, track, and validate offers. Some offers are operated by independent advertisers and are subject to their own privacy practices and terms. Use of the offerwall is voluntary; users who do not open it are not subject to its data collection.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information. Information collected by the SDKs listed above is processed by their respective providers, who act as independent controllers or processors of that data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob & Firebase Analytics – https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Torox – https://torox.io/privacy-policy

7. Children's Privacy

Our games are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in the user's jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

8. Data Retention

Data collected by the third-party SDKs is retained according to each provider's retention practices. We do not maintain our own database of user personal data.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, object to its processing, or withdraw consent. Because we rely on third-party SDKs for data processing, requests may be directed to those providers, or you can contact us using the details below and we will assist where possible.

10. Data Security

We aim to maintain reasonable technical and organizational measures to keep our applications secure and reliable. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is fully secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. When we make updates, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
